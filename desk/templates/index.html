  • ▲Resized or re-cropped derivatives keep their metadata but may fall below the pixel-detection threshold. Derive from sealed masters.
  • ▲Extreme high-frequency texture can hide a 5 L* relief from re-detection. The relief is still applied and the metadata still discloses.
  • ▲Metadata is unsigned and strippable, and many platforms strip it on upload. C2PA signing is the answer if tamper evidence is required.
  • ▲CMYK input is converted to RGB and flagged in the manifest. Seal the RGB master and let prepress convert.
  • ▲This implements marking practice. Whether an asset triggers EU AI Act Art. 50 duties, and in which role, is a question for counsel.

Masters only, one seal version in circulation, and the manifest kept forever. The visible seal and the embedded provenance always ship together: one without the other fails the standard. Storage lives on the mounted volume, so a redeploy without a volume loses the originals and the audit trail.
